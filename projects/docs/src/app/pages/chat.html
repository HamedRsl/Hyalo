Try the conversation

Search or filter conversations, then use Actions, right-click, or Shift+F10 on a message’s Actions button to reply, react, forward, or copy. You can edit and delete your local drafts.

Import the primitives

Application boundaries

The demo keeps messages, drafts, reactions and attachments in this tab until you leave the page. Attach a photo to crop or rotate it before adding it to the conversation. Pending and failed delivery are explicit previews. Record voice uses your browser’s microphone permission. Add your own transport, storage, and delivery acknowledgements when integrating the example.

Keyboard and reading order

The conversation list and history are keyboard-scrollable. Enter adds a line in the composer. Use Add local draft to submit. Selecting a quoted reply jumps to its original message when it is available.
