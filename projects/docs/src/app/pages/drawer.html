Accessibility

  • Same behaviour as a dialog: focus trap, Escape, backdrop click and focus restore.
  • Give it a title with hyDialogTitle or a label.
  • Drag it toward its edge or flick it to close it, and grab it again while it moves. A bottom sheet shows a grabber.
  • With reduced motion it cross-fades in place instead of sliding, and is not dragged.

API

HyDrawer.open(content, config) returns a HyDialogRef. It takes every dialog option except size, plus these.
