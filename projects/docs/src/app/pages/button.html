Accessibility

  • Icon-only buttons need an aria-label.
  • While loading, the button keeps focus and its width, sets aria-busy and ignores clicks.
  • Links have no native disabled state, so a disabled link gets aria-disabled and leaves the tab order.
  • Every size keeps a target of at least 32 by 32 pixels.
