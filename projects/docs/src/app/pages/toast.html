Behaviour

  • Up to four are visible. A fifth one pushes the oldest out.
  • Timers pause while the pointer is over the stack or focus is inside it.
  • A toast with an action stays until dismissed, so nobody has to race a timer to press Undo.
  • Swipe a toast sideways to dismiss it on touch screens.

Accessibility

  • Messages go through a live region. Danger toasts are assertive, the rest are polite.
  • The stack is a labelled region, so screen reader users can find it later.
  • Never put the only copy of important information in a toast. It goes away.
