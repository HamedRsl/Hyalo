Behavior and accessibility

The dock reuses tab-bar link semantics and keyboard navigation. Magnification is opt-in and limited to fine hover pointers; touch, reduced motion and low fidelity keep stable icons. Link hit targets never scale. Fixed positioning follows logical edges and safe areas. Leave room for fixed chrome in the application layout.

Design tokens

Uses shared surface, ink, spacing, radius, focus, semantic tone and motion tokens. Override local component variables on the host; reduced motion, forced colors and fidelity retain functional controls.
