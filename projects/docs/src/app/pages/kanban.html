Behavior and accessibility

Move handles support touch delay and preserve scrolling outside the handle. Earlier/later buttons and column/lane selects provide the same moves without dragging. WIP limits and canMove apply to every path. A pending approval locks moves; a replaced source, cancellation, or destruction prevents stale writes. moved reports the accepted proposal after an immutable columns update. Filtering disables pointer reordering because hidden cards have ambiguous drop positions.
