Behavior and accessibility

Events are exposed as a list and use native time elements. Date formatting belongs to the application, so Persian and Jalali display dates are supported without conversion. Logical borders mirror the thread in RTL. Horizontal timelines support start, end and alternate alignment along the same rail. Use side on an event to override its placement.
