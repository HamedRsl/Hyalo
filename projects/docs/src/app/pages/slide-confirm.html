Behavior and accessibility

Only actual travel past the configured fraction confirms; a fast short flick does not. Pointer cancellation restores the start. Click, Enter and Space confirm through the native handle button. Loading, readonly and disabled prevent changes. This boolean Signal Forms control reports touch, supports field metadata and keeps a stable accessible name.
