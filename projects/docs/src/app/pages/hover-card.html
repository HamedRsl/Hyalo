Behavior and accessibility

Hover waits briefly; focus and touch open immediately. The card stays open while its content is hovered or focused and Escape dismisses it. Press Arrow Down on the trigger to move focus into the card. Keep essential information visible elsewhere. Use a popover for forms and tasks requiring deliberate focus transfer.
