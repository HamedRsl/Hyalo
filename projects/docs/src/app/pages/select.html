Native fallback

On the low fidelity tier, or with the native input, the component renders a real <select> with the same look. Phones then get their own picker, which is often the better experience for long lists.

Accessibility

  • Follows the APG select-only combobox pattern. Enter, Space or Down open the list.
  • Typing a letter jumps to the next option that starts with it.
  • Escape closes without changing the value. Focus stays on the trigger the whole time.
  • For more than about 15 options, or when people need to search, use a combobox instead. It is planned for phase 4.
