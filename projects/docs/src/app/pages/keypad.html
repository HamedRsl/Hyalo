Behavior and accessibility

Persian and Arabic digits normalize to ASCII; values stay strings, preserving leading zeros. Native typing, paste, composition, backspace and keypad buttons share validation. Decimal and negative entry are opt-in. Max length includes sign and separator. Masking changes presentation and does not secure payment or PIN data.
