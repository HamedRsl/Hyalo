Behavior and accessibility

Persian and Arabic digits normalize to ASCII; values stay strings, preserving leading zeros. Native typing, paste, composition, backspace and keypad buttons share validation. Decimal and negative entry are opt-in. Max length includes sign and separator. Masking changes presentation and does not secure payment or PIN data. This is a Signal Forms value control, with shared field metadata and touch/focus behavior.

Design tokens

Uses shared surface, ink, spacing, radius, focus, semantic tone and motion tokens. Override local component variables on the host; reduced motion, forced colors and fidelity retain functional controls.
