Behavior and accessibility

Highest finite priority wins; equal priorities keep arrival order. Controlled items emit dismiss requests. Without items, the root HyStatusQueue service owns a local queue through set, remove and clear. The stable native trigger opens a focus-managed popover and remains present when the queue empties. Announcements describe status changes rather than every progress frame.

Design tokens

Uses shared surface, ink, spacing, radius, focus, semantic tone and motion tokens. Override local component variables on the host; reduced motion, forced colors and fidelity retain functional controls.
