Behavior and accessibility

With no actions, activated emits for a single FAB. With actions, ArrowUp or ArrowDown opens and focuses the first enabled action; arrows, Home and End move focus. Escape closes and restores focus. Linear, circle, semicircle and quarter-circle layouts share the same data model. Mask and outside dismissal are optional. Keep radial groups inside a viewport area large enough for their configured radius.

Design tokens

Uses shared surface, ink, spacing, radius, focus, semantic tone and motion tokens. Override local component variables on the host; reduced motion, forced colors and fidelity retain functional controls.
