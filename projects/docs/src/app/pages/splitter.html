Behavior and accessibility

Focus a separator and use arrows, Home, or End to resize; Enter toggles the preceding collapsible pane. Horizontal arrows follow RTL. Escape, pointercancel, and capture loss restore the gesture start. All initial, external, imperative, collapse, and restore sizes share the same min/max constraints. Impossible allocations are rejected, and collapsed panes receive zero space. Resize events identify pointer, keyboard, or API sources.
