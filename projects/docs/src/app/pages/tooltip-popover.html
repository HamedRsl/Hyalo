Which one

Tooltips are for a few words that name or explain a control. They never hold links or buttons, and they do not show on touch. If the content is interactive, or longer than a sentence, use a popover.

Accessibility

  • Tooltip text is added to the trigger with aria-describedby, so screen readers hear it right away.
  • Tooltips show on hover after a short delay and on keyboard focus at once. Escape hides them.
  • A popover is a non-modal dialog. Focus moves to its first control and returns to the trigger on close.
  • Escape, a click outside, or tabbing out closes the popover.
