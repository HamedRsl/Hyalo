Behavior and accessibility

Bind value directly or use Signal Forms through formField. Editing leaves the committed value alone until Apply. Cancel restores it; Clear sets it to null. Translate the labels and validationMessage for your application.

The date-only and time-only controls keep their existing formats. This component combines them into an instant in the supplied timezone. UTC is the deterministic default. Supply the initial value and timezone from the host for SSR.

Skipped local times cannot be applied. Repeated times default to the earlier instant; choose later or reject through disambiguation. Exact min and max bounds are checked after resolving the instant.

Pass HyJalaliDateAdapter, or provide HY_DATE_ADAPTER in a parent, to display the same instant in a Jalali calendar.
